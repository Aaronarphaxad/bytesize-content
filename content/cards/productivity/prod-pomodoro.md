ByteSize works as a deliberate ten-minute ritual. A timer turns feed energy into a closed loop that leaves you measurably smarter.

## Detail

The mechanics that make social feeds compulsive — variable reward, infinite supply, zero friction — are neutral technology. Point them at micro-learning and the same pull produces compounding knowledge instead of residue.

The missing piece in social apps is a stopping cue. Set one deliberately: a timer, a card count, or a single commute. Ten focused minutes daily beats a ninety-minute binge you then avoid for a fortnight.

Anchor it to something you already do without fail — coffee, standup, the train. Habit formation is mostly about reliable cues rather than motivation, which is why the time of day matters more than the duration.
